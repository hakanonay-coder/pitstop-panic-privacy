Privacy Policy for PitStop Panic

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

1. Introduction

PitStop Panic ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile game.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Game progress, scores, levels completed, in-game actions
  • Advertising ID: Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) for personalized advertising
  • Crash and Performance Data: Technical information to improve app stability

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and improving the game experience
  • Displaying personalized advertisements through Unity Ads
  • Analyzing game performance and user engagement
  • Fixing bugs and technical issues
  • Saving your game progress locally on your device

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

Unity Ads

We use Unity Ads to display advertisements in the game. Unity Ads may collect and process information including device identifiers, IP address, and advertising data.

  • Unity Ads Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity Ads Data Processing: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

5. Data Storage and Security

Your game progress is stored locally on your device. We do not store personal information on our servers. However, third-party services like Unity Ads may store data according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Our game is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights:

  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can disable personalized advertising through your iOS device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable for PitStop Panic)
  • Delete Data: You can delete all game data by deleting the app from your device
  • Request Information: Contact us to request information about data we have collected

8. International Users

If you are located outside of Turkey, your information may be transferred to and processed in Turkey or other countries where Unity Ads operates. By using our game, you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
